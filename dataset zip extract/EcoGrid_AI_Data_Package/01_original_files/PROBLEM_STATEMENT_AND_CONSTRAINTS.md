# Problem Statement: Predictive Circular-Resource Grid for a Multi-Building Campus

A campus spanning multiple buildings (academic blocks, hostels, cafeteria, labs) generates heterogeneous, time-varying streams of waste, water, and electricity consumption. Facilities teams currently rely on manual, building-by-building audits that lag real consumption by days, cannot forecast demand spikes (exam season, events, peak meal hours), and cannot attribute waste/energy usage to specific causes — making optimization and accountability nearly impossible.

Design and prototype an AI-driven "Predictive Circular-Resource Grid" that satisfies ALL of the following constraints simultaneously:

* **Multi-modal sensor fusion under noise:** Fuse at least three heterogeneous, noisy, and asynchronous data sources (e.g., low-cost camera frames, load/flow/energy-meter sensors, and manually logged entries) sampled at different rates, and resolve conflicting or missing readings without discarding buildings that temporarily go offline.
* **Real-time classification at the edge:** Classify waste/consumption events into at least 5 categories with a target accuracy above 85%, running inference on low-cost, resource-constrained edge hardware (no continuous cloud GPU access) within 2 seconds per event.
* **Short-horizon forecasting, not just monitoring:** Predict building-level demand or waste-generation spikes at least 6–12 hours ahead (e.g., before a mess rush or exam-week surge) using time-series/ML techniques, and explicitly justify the accuracy–compute trade-off chosen for edge deployment.
* **Intermittent connectivity & data integrity:** Operate correctly when individual buildings lose network connectivity for hours at a time, queue/buffer data locally, and reconcile conflicting or delayed updates once connectivity is restored — without corrupting the campus-wide dashboard.
* **Root-cause attribution, not just dashboards:** Go beyond raw graphs to attribute anomalies to a probable cause (e.g., a specific block, shift, or event) and generate a ranked, explainable action recommendation — the system must justify why it made each recommendation.
* **Behavioural + economic incentive loop:** Design a closed-loop mechanism (gamification, credits, leaderboards, or dynamic reporting) that measurably nudges building occupants toward reducing waste/consumption, and define how its impact would be measured.
* **Cost & scale envelope:** The full proof-of-concept (sensors + compute + software) must be designed to fit within a stated per-building hardware budget of the team's choosing (state and justify it), and the architecture must be shown, with a concrete scaling argument, to extend from 3 buildings to 30+ without a fundamental redesign.
* **Failure-mode awareness:** Teams must identify at least two realistic failure modes of their own system (e.g., sensor drift, adversarial gaming of the incentive system, model degradation over a semester) and propose a concrete mitigation for each.
